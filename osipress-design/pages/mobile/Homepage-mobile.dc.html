You’ve never read an Iranian newspaper.

Probably not an Israeli one either. Most of what you know about the Iran–Israel war reached you through outlets in your own country, written in your language for readers like you. OsiPress, the Open Source Info Press, translates what foreign newspapers publish for their own readers each day and sets it beside the original.

On 20 June, Iran declared the Strait of Hormuz closed, again.

A fifth of the world’s oil passes through that channel, so newspapers in both countries led with it that morning. In Tasnim, Israel has broken a ceasefire in southern Lebanon and the strait is closing in response. In Israel Hayom, the army is rounding up Hezbollah fighters and the closure is only a threat.

Tasnim, Iran20 June 2026

Neither paper is lying; they simply begin the story in different places. OsiPress does the reading you couldn’t do alone, in the original languages with every source linked, and leaves the conclusions to you.
