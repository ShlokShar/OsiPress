You’ve never read an Iranian newspaper.

Probably not an Israeli one either. Most of what you know about the Iran–Israel war reached you through outlets in your own country, written in your language for readers like you. OsiPress, the Open Source Info Press, translates what foreign newspapers publish for their own readers each day and sets it beside the original, so you can read the story the way it was told there.

Every country, read across its own politics.

For each country we follow outlets that run the length of its political spectrum, from reformist to hardline in Iran and from left to right in Israel. Those scales mean different things in different places, so each country gets its own axis, and every outlet is shown where its readers would place it.
